Transit Cards & Smart Apps

Download these before you fly for a seamless, cashless, and stress-free trip.

EasyCard (悠遊卡) Essential

The absolute essential. Works on MRT, TRA trains, buses, convenience stores (7-11, FamilyMart), and YouBike.

Where to buy

Ticket vending machines right before the MRT gates at Airport Terminal 1/2. Card fee is NT$100 (~S$4.15) plus top up (start with NT$500).

Uber / FindTaxi

Uber works perfectly in Taipei (billed in NTD to your card). FindTaxi (呼叫小黃) is a great local alternative that calls metered yellow cabs if Uber surge pricing is high.

Hidden Costs Audit

Keep these in mind while budgeting. (Calculated at approx S$1 = NT$24).

Plastic Bag Fee

Convenience stores and supermarkets charge NT$1-NT$3 (~S$0.05-0.15) per bag. Bring a tote!

10% Service Charge

Most sit-down restaurants (like Din Tai Fung) add a mandatory 10% service charge to your bill. Tipping beyond this is NOT expected.

Cafe Minimum Spend

Many aesthetic cafes have a "Minimum One Drink per Person" rule, usually around NT$150 (~S$6.25), even if you order desserts.

Night Market Cash

90% of night market stalls and old street vendors strictly take cash only. Always have NT$100 notes and coins ready.
