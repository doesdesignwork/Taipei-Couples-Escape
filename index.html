Where to buy

Uber / FindTaxi

Uber works perfectly. FindTaxi (呼叫小黃) is a great local alternative for yellow cabs.

Hidden Costs Audit

Cafe Minimum Spend

Many cafes have a "One Drink per Person" rule, usually NT$150 (~S$6.25).

Night Market Cash

90% of night markets are strictly cash only.
